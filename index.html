About the organisation

Creating a credible pipeline from school sport to elite opportunity.

Unicorn Sports Limited is a registered sports management company in Nigeria established to identify, develop, manage and promote sporting talent through structured school sports programs. Schools are a crucial environment for discovering young talent, but many capable athletes do not reach their full potential due to limited competition, poor exposure, and a lack of structured pathways.

The organisation addresses this by combining competition, athlete assessment, safeguarding, educational accountability, and talent pathway management into a consistent, year-round model.

Vision & objectives

Building a structured school sports ecosystem.

Vision

To build a structured school sports ecosystem in Nigeria that identifies young talents early, develops their abilities systematically, and creates credible pathways to local and international sporting opportunities.

✓

Core objectives

  • Establish regular and organised school sporting competitions.
  • Promote fair competition across age and gender categories.
  • Identify athletes for further training and assessment.
  • Create opportunities for scholarships and international pathways.

Termly organisation model

A connected sports development journey across the academic year.

Term 1

Participation, screening and competitive assessment

  • Invite schools and register team entries.
  • Verify athlete age eligibility and school status.
  • Organise competitions across age categories and gender groups.
  • Assess physical readiness, technical ability and attitude.

Expected outcome: a registered pool of schools and athletes with reliable early records.

Term 2

Competitive development and talent identification

  • Monitor athlete development through match performances.
  • Review team and individual performance data.
  • Identify standout players for specialist assessment.
  • Explore academy, scholarship and international development opportunities.

Expected outcome: improved competitive experience and a shortlist of promising athletes.

Term 3

Performance evaluation, championships and pathway development

  • Organise concluding competitions and championship events.
  • Review athlete performance throughout the academic year.
  • Recommend suitable athletes for academy trials or scholarship consideration.
  • Recognise outstanding athletes, coaches and schools.

Expected outcome: a documented season review and a structured pathway plan for the next cycle.

Age-category structure

Fair and age-appropriate competition.

Primary

Under-8, U10, U12

Focus on participation, fundamentals, confidence, discipline and early talent identification.

Junior Secondary

U13 and U14

Emphasis on technical assessment, tactical awareness, competitive discipline and game intelligence.

Senior Secondary

U15, U16, U17

Performance consistency, physical preparation, psychological readiness, and exposure to higher-level pathways.
